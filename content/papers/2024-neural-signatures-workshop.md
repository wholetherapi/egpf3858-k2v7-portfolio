# Neural Signatures for Program Semantics

Christina Adams, Priya Raman

## Abstract

Workshop version. We study whether a learned signature can retrieve
semantically equivalent functions produced by different compiler optimisation
levels in a single language. The extended journal version broadens the study
to multiple languages and adds the transfer experiments.

## Scope

- Single language (C), two optimisation levels.
- Small hand-curated retrieval set.
- No cross-language evaluation.
