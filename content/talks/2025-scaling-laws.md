# What Scaling Laws Actually Tell Us

Invited seminar, MLSys Community Seminar 2025.

## Outline

1. What the fitted curves predict, and under which assumptions.
2. Where extrapolation breaks: data mixture shifts, architecture changes and
   inference-budget coupling.
3. A checklist for using scaling estimates in planning.

## Takeaways

- Treat scaling estimates as a planning aid, not a guarantee.
- Re-fit after every material change to the data mixture.
- Report the fitted regime alongside any extrapolated number.
